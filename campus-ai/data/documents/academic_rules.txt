SECTION 1: COURSE REGISTRATION & DEADLINE EXCEPTIONS
[Page 8]
1.1 Add/Drop Period & Registration Deadlines
Course registration takes place during the first week of every semester. The official Add/Drop deadline is 11:59 PM on the second Friday following the start of classes.
- After the Add/Drop deadline, no automated course adjustments are permitted on the portal.

[Page 9]
1.2 Missed Deadline Protocol & Late Registration Requests
Students who have missed the formal deadline due to documented extenuating circumstances (such as medical hospitalization or bereavement) must:
1. Obtain an Academic Exception Form (Form AC-10) from their Department Academic Office.
2. Secure the written endorsement and recommendation of the Department Head (HOD) and the Course Faculty Advisor.
3. Submit the completed application with supporting evidence to the Dean of Academic Affairs (Administrative Building, 2nd Floor, Room 204).
4. Late registration petitions incur a standard late administrative fee of $25 and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

[Page 10]
1.3 Minimum Attendance Requirement
A minimum attendance of 75% in lecture, tutorial, and laboratory sessions is compulsory to be eligible for end-semester examinations. Medical leave condonations up to 10% may be sanctioned upon submission of a certified medical certificate within seven (7) days of resuming classes.
