SECTION 1: EXAMINATION CREDENTIALS & HALL ADMISSION
[Page 15]
1.1 Hall Ticket / Examination Admit Card
Every candidate must present their physical Examination Admit Card (Hall Ticket) along with their valid Student ID Card at the entrance of the examination hall.
- Admit cards are downloadable from the Student Portal starting one week prior to exams.
- If a student loses their Hall Ticket, a duplicate Hall Ticket can be generated at the Controller of Examinations Helpdesk (Exam Building, Room 12) upon payment of a $10 fee and verification of roll number.

[Page 16]
1.2 Examination Timings & Permitted Materials
Candidates are required to be seated fifteen (15) minutes before the scheduled commencement of the examination. No student is admitted into the examination hall after thirty (30) minutes of commencement.
- Electronic gadgets, smart watches, and programmable calculators are strictly prohibited inside the hall.

[Page 17]
1.3 Grade Re-evaluation & Scrutiny Process
Students seeking re-evaluation of answer scripts must apply through the examination portal within fourteen (14) calendar days of the formal declaration of semester results. The re-evaluation fee is $30 per course.
