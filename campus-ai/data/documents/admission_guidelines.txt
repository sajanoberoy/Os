SECTION 1: PHYSICAL DOCUMENT VERIFICATION
[Page 4]
1.1 Verification Venue & Schedule
All newly admitted undergraduate and postgraduate students must complete mandatory physical document verification before course enrollment is finalized.
- Venue: Central Academic Complex, Multi-Purpose Hall (Ground Floor, Wing C).
- Verification Window: Monday through Friday, 09:00 AM to 04:00 PM (Lunch break: 01:00 PM to 02:00 PM).

[Page 5]
1.2 Mandatory Document Checklist
Candidates must present original copies along with three (3) sets of self-attested photocopies of the following credentials:
1. University Provisional Admission Letter / Offer Slip
2. High School (10th Standard) Certificate and Marksheet (for Date of Birth verification)
3. Higher Secondary (12th Standard / Pre-University) Marksheet and Passing Certificate
4. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct/Character Certificate from the previous institution
5. Official Migration Certificate (for students from other boards or universities)
6. Category/Reservation Certificate (if applicable: SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Disability)
7. Four (4) recent passport-sized color photographs with white background
8. Government-issued photo identification (Passport, National ID, or Driver's License)
9. Official University Tuition Fee Payment Challan/Receipt

[Page 6]
1.3 Provisional Approval & Deficiencies
If any non-critical certificate (such as Migration Certificate) is pending at the time of verification, candidate may submit an Undertaking Form (Form A-3) granting a conditional grace period of fourteen (14) days to produce the original certificate.
