SECTION 1: STUDENT IDENTIFICATION CARDS & CREDENTIALS
[Page 12]
1.1 Issuance of Identity Cards
Every enrolled student is issued a secure photographic Student Identification Card (ID Card) upon completion of admission verification. The ID Card serves as the primary credential for physical campus entry, access to the Central Library, laboratory facilities, examination halls, and cafeteria services.

[Page 13]
1.2 Lost or Damaged ID Cards — Official Replacement Procedure
In the event of a lost, stolen, or damaged Student ID Card, the student is required to follow the formal replacement protocol:
1. File an official Loss Report at the Office of Student Affairs (Administrative Building, Block A, Room 104) or submit the online Loss Notification form via the Student Academic Portal.
2. Pay the mandatory replacement processing fee of $15 (or local currency equivalent) at the University Cashier's Counter (Window 2, Ground Floor, Administrative Building).
3. Present the official payment receipt along with one passport-sized photograph and a government-issued photo ID (Passport, National Identity Card, or Driver's License) to the Student Credentials Desk in Room 104.
4. Standard processing time for a new permanent physical ID Card is three to five (3-5) working business days.

[Page 14]
1.3 Temporary Campus Access Permit
While waiting for a replacement ID Card, students may obtain a Temporary Campus Pass valid for seven (7) days from the Security Desk at Main Gate 1 or Gate 2 by presenting their provisional fee receipt and identity verification.

SECTION 2: CAMPUS SECURITY & ACCESS REGULATIONS
[Page 22]
2.1 Campus Entry Hours
Main gates are open for student entry from 06:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays and 07:00 AM to 09:00 PM on weekends. Entry beyond designated hours requires prior written authorization from the Dean of Student Welfare.

[Page 23]

- Student Welfare & Counseling Office: Admin Block B, Room 208 (09:00 AM - 05:00 PM)
- Medical & Health Center: Health Wellness Building Ground Floor (24/7 emergency service)
